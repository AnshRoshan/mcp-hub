# Meeting Notes

Use this skill whenever asked to summarize a transcript, take notes from a meeting, or turn a discussion into action items.

## What to capture

- **Decisions** — what was settled, and who settles it if it wasn't. A decision without the reasoning is hard to revisit later.
- **Action items** — each with an owner and a due date or milestone. An action item with no owner is a wish.
- **Open questions** — things explicitly deferred, with who owns the follow-up.
- **Context others need** — background an absent teammate would miss. Keep it brief.
- **Skip the transcript.** Do not reproduce the conversation; summarize what changed as a result of it.

## Structure

```markdown
# <Meeting title> — <date>
**Attendees:** ...   **Outcome:** one line

## Decisions
- **X was decided.** Why: ...

## Action items
- [ ] Owner — task, by <date>
- [ ] Owner — task, by <date>

## Open questions
- ...
```

## Rules

- Lead with the **outcome**, then decisions, then actions — a reader should get the point in the first two lines.
- Attribute actions and decisions to people, never to "the team".
- Flag anything that sounds like a commitment made without agreement ("sounds like X is now on the roadmap — confirm?").
